## 📋 Professional Design Elements for Delivery Company

**What Makes a Professional Delivery Fleet Logo:**

1. **Typography Hierarchy:**
   - PRUSH: Bold, large, orange (primary)
   - LOGISTICS GROUP: Regular weight, smaller, grey (secondary)
   - Clear size difference creates visual hierarchy

2. **Color Consistency:**
   - Orange (#EA580C): Primary brand color, conveys energy/speed
   - Dark grey (#666666): Professional, readable, secondary
   - White background: Clean, professional

3. **Readability at Distance:**
   - Large enough to read from 50+ feet
   - Proper letter spacing (not too tight)
   - High contrast (orange on white)

4. **Fleet Uniformity:**
   - Identical branding on all vehicles
   - Same placement, sizing, colors
   - Professional corporate appearance

5. **Commercial Appeal:**
   - Clean, modern design
   - Conveys reliability and professionalism
   - Suitable for dealership and commercial clients
