# AI Logo & Decal Design Prompts for PRUSH LOGISTICS GROUP

## Goal
Generate professional logo and decal designs for a delivery/logistics fleet company.

---

## Prompt 1: Professional Fleet Logo Design (Detailed)

```
Professional delivery fleet photo showing three white commercial cargo vans with PRUSH LOGISTICS GROUP branding applied to their side panels.

VEHICLES:
- Two white Nissan NV2500 High-Roof cargo vans
- One white Dodge ProMaster cargo van
- All three vehicles parked side-by-side, fully visible in frame
- Professional fleet photography, even lighting

BRANDING ON EACH VAN:
- Main logo "PRUSH" in large, bold, sans-serif font, burnt orange color (#EA580C), positioned on upper-middle section of side panel
- The letter 'H' in "PRUSH" features a right-pointing arrow seamlessly integrated into the right vertical stroke, creating a unified orange element that suggests speed and forward movement
- Below "PRUSH", smaller text "LOGISTICS GROUP" in dark charcoal grey (#666666), regular weight, approximately 40-45% of PRUSH height, center-aligned
- Slogan "We put the rush in Prush" displayed below LOGISTICS GROUP, with "rush" and "Prush" in burnt orange, rest in dark charcoal grey
- Curved orange stripe running along lower side panel, following vehicle contours

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, modern, professional commercial fleet branding
- High contrast for visibility at distance
- Professional logistics company aesthetic
- Consistent branding across all three vehicles
- Bold, readable typography
- Dynamic arrow element conveys speed and efficiency

COLOR SCHEME:
- Primary: Burnt orange (#EA580C)
- Secondary: Dark charcoal grey (#666666)
- Vehicle color: White

LAYOUT:
- Branding applied to side panels of all three vehicles
- Clear visual hierarchy: PRUSH (primary), LOGISTICS GROUP (secondary), slogan (tertiary)
- Professional spacing and alignment
- Identical branding on all vehicles for fleet consistency
```

## 📝 Notes for AI Generation

**What to look for in results:**
- ✅ Arrow seamlessly integrated into H
- ✅ Clear hierarchy (PRUSH largest, LOGISTICS GROUP smaller)
- ✅ Professional appearance
- ✅ Good contrast and readability
- ✅ Suitable for vehicle decals
- ✅ Includes slogan
- ✅ Orange and dark grey colors

**What to avoid:**
- ❌ Too busy or cluttered
- ❌ Poor readability
- ❌ Arrow that looks separate from H
- ❌ Inconsistent sizing
- ❌ Unprofessional appearance

---

## 🎨 Style Variations to Try

**If you want different aesthetics, add these to prompts:**

- **"Modern minimalist"** - Clean, simple
- **"Bold commercial"** - High-impact, strong
- **"Professional corporate"** - Traditional, established
- **"Dynamic speed-focused"** - Motion, energy
- **"Geometric modern"** - Clean lines, shapes
- **"Classic professional"** - Timeless, reliable
