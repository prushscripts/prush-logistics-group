## Prompt 1: Detailed & Explicit (Try This First)

```
Edit this image: Three white delivery vans with PRUSH LOGISTICS GROUP branding. 

ARROW FIX:
- On the leftmost and middle vans, make the orange arrow on the H seamlessly blend into the right vertical stroke of the H, exactly like the rightmost van. The arrow should appear as one unified orange element with no white gap or separation. The arrow should flow naturally from the H, creating a professional, integrated look.

TEXT FIXES:
- Make "LOGISTICS GROUP" text smaller on all three vans - it should be 40-45% of the PRUSH text height (currently it's too large).
- Ensure "LOGISTICS GROUP" is spelled correctly (all caps, no typos).
- Center-align "LOGISTICS GROUP" with "PRUSH" text above it, with tight spacing (10-15px gap).
- Keep "LOGISTICS GROUP" in dark charcoal grey color.

CONSISTENCY:
- Ensure all three vans have identical branding elements.
- Keep the curved orange stripe on all vans.
- Keep the slogan "We put the rush in Prush" on the rightmost van (it's already correct).
- If other vans are partially visible, apply branding to visible portions.

STYLING:
- Maintain professional, clean appearance.
- Orange color: #EA580C (burnt orange).
- Dark charcoal grey for LOGISTICS GROUP: #666666.
- All text should be crisp and readable.
```
