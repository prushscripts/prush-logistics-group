# Fixing the Arrow - Step by Step Guide

## ❌ Problem: Healing Brush is Smudging, Not Removing

**Why this happens:**
- Healing Brush is designed to **blend**, not completely remove
- It tries to match surrounding colors/textures
- For complete removal, you need a different method

---

## ✅ SOLUTION: Remove Arrow Completely (3 Methods)

### **Method 1: Content-Aware Fill (BEST - Removes Completely)**

1. **Select the arrow area:**
   - **Lasso Tool** (L key)
   - **Draw around the arrow** (just the arrow, not the H)
   - Make sure selection includes all of the arrow

2. **Expand selection slightly:**
   - **Select → Modify → Expand**
   - Enter **2-3 pixels**
   - This helps with blending

3. **Fill with Content-Aware:**
   - **Edit → Fill** (or Shift+F5)
   - Choose **Content-Aware** from dropdown
   - Click **OK**

4. **Result:** Arrow completely gone, replaced with white van color!

**This is the BEST method** - no smudging, clean removal!

---

### **Method 2: Paint White Over It (Simple & Direct)**

1. **Select Brush Tool** (B key)

2. **Get exact white color:**
   - **Eyedropper Tool** (I key)
   - **Click on clean white van area** (away from logo)
   - This samples the exact white color

3. **Paint over arrow:**
   - **Brush Tool** (B key)
   - **Soft brush** (0-20% hardness)
   - **Paint directly over the arrow**
   - Cover it completely with white

4. **Result:** Arrow painted over with matching white!

---

### **Method 3: Clone Stamp (If Others Don't Work)**

1. **Select Clone Stamp Tool** (S key)

2. **Sample white area:**
   - **Hold Alt**, click on **clean white van area**
   - Make sure you're sampling from area with similar lighting

3. **Paint over arrow:**
   - **Click and drag** over the arrow
   - Cover it completely
   - Resample (Alt+click) if needed

## 🎯 Should You Remove the Whole PRUSH Text First?

### **NO - Keep the PRUSH Text!**

**Why:**
- You need PRUSH text as a **reference** to know where to place the arrow
- The arrow should **extend from the right side of the H**
- If you remove PRUSH first, you won't know where to position the arrow correctly

**Correct Workflow:**
1. ✅ **Keep PRUSH text visible**
2. ✅ **Remove ONLY the old arrow** (using Method 1 above)
3. ✅ **Create new arrow** that blends into the H
4. ✅ **Position arrow** extending from right side of H

**The PRUSH text stays - you're just fixing the arrow part!**

## 📋 Complete Arrow Fix Workflow

### Step 1: Remove Old Arrow
1. **Lasso Tool** (L key)
2. **Select arrow area only** (not the H)
3. **Select → Modify → Expand → 2 pixels**
4. **Edit → Fill → Content-Aware → OK**
5. ✅ Arrow completely gone!

### Step 2: Create New Arrow (Image 1 Style)
1. **Create new layer** "Arrow Fix"
2. **Zoom in** on the H (Ctrl + Plus)
3. **Select Brush Tool** (B key)
4. **Set color:** #EA580C (orange)
5. **Brush size:** Match H stroke width
6. **Hardness:** 0% (soft edges)
7. **Draw arrow:**
   - Start from **right vertical stroke of H**
   - Draw **horizontal line** extending right
   - Draw **arrow point** at end
8. **Blend into H:**
   - **Eraser Tool** (E key)
   - **Soft brush** (0% hardness)
   - **Erase edges** where arrow meets H
   - Make it look **seamless and unified**

### Step 3: Verify
- ✅ Arrow blends seamlessly into H
- ✅ No visible seam or separation
- ✅ Arrow is same orange as PRUSH (#EA580C)
- ✅ Looks like one unified element
