## Specific Arrow Fix Instructions

### What's Wrong:
- Arrow looks separate/attached to H (not integrated)
- White horizontal line visible (probably the H's crossbar showing through)
- Arrow doesn't flow naturally from H

### What Should Happen:
- Arrow should be **part of the H itself**
- The right vertical stroke of H should **extend into the arrow**
- **No white lines or gaps** - one continuous orange element
- Arrow should look like it **grows from the H**, not attached to it

### Detailed Arrow Fix Prompt:
```
The letter H in PRUSH should have its right vertical stroke extend horizontally to the right, forming an arrow. The arrow is not a separate element - it's the H's right stroke continuing outward. Remove the white horizontal crossbar of the H where it meets the arrow extension. The entire H-arrow should be one continuous orange shape with no white gaps, seams, or separate elements. The arrow point should flow naturally from the extended right stroke of the H.
```
